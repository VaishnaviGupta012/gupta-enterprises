Design a complete, modern, professional, responsive website UI/UX for a local digital service business named:

“GUPTA ENTERPRISES”

Business Type:
Digital & Online Service Assistance Centre / CSC-style service centre.

IMPORTANT:
This is currently a UI/UX design task only. Do not focus on backend development.
The website must NOT falsely imply that Gupta Enterprises is an official government website or government department.
Use terms such as “Digital Service Centre”, “Online Service Assistance”, and “Application Assistance” wherever appropriate.
Do not use government logos or claim official authorization unless explicitly provided.

=====================================
1. DESIGN GOAL
=====================================

Create a trustworthy, clean and easy-to-use website where customers can:

• Discover available services
• Search for a particular service
• Browse services by category
• Understand what each service is for
• Check required documents
• Understand the application process
• Contact the centre
• Call directly
• Send a WhatsApp enquiry
• Find the physical centre/location
• View FAQs
• See opening hours

The primary users may not be highly technical, so make the interface extremely simple, readable and mobile-friendly.

The website should feel like a professional Indian digital service centre, but NOT like an outdated government portal.

=====================================
2. VISUAL STYLE
=====================================

Create a premium but simple design.

Style:
• Modern
• Professional
• Trustworthy
• Minimal
• Clean
• Friendly
• Accessible
• Mobile-first

Color palette:
• Deep Navy / Royal Blue as primary
• White background
• Very light blue/grey section backgrounds
• Green only for WhatsApp/success elements
• Dark charcoal text
• Subtle accent colors for service categories

Avoid:
• Too many bright colors
• Heavy gradients
• Cluttered government-portal appearance
• Excessive text
• Oversized animations
• Cheap-looking clipart
• Excessive shadows

Use:
• Rounded cards
• Subtle shadows
• Clean line icons
• Good spacing
• Clear typography hierarchy
• Large clickable buttons
• Consistent border radius
• Modern illustrations/icons where useful

Typography:
Use a modern readable font such as Inter, Poppins or similar.
